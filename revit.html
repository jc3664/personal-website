Revit Experience

As part of my Intro to Revit course, I followed a series of guided exercises to model the interior and exterior of a three-story building. The building features a central atrium with a curtain wall system, along with multiple floors of office space. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience with Revit's modeling tools and learned how different building components come together in a complete architectural model.

Another Revit project is coming soon! For my next project, I will be designing a building independently, applying the Revit tools and techniques I learned through textbook exercises to develop a complete building model of my own.
